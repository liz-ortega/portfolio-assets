01 · context

mobilizing safely in a high-risk world.

Nodo was developed during the LA-Tech Rising Internship around one prompt: how might we use AI and research to safely empower and sustain activists? As the sole designer on a six-person team, I bridged business strategy, AI integration and user privacy.

The LA-Tech Rising final showcase.
02 · the challenge Organizers want lasting impact, but their tools put them at risk.

Grassroots organizers face legal risk, surveillance, misinformation and burnout, all without secure tools built for how they actually work.

03 · the shift

from scattered to secure.

fragmented tools, exposed data → one secure home for organizing.

Centralize everything an organizer needs under one encrypted platform, and give people real control over their data and their roles.

04 · research

what organizers told us.

Interviews with coordinators, student activists and volunteers surfaced the critical needs that became Nodo's design principles.

INSIGHT 01 fragmentation drains capacity Principle: Centralize organization under one secure platform.
INSIGHT 02 emotional safety equals technical safety Principle: Foster digital trust through transparency and control.
INSIGHT 03 safety is non-negotiable Principle: Protect user data and inform safe organizing.
INSIGHT 04 accessibility builds inclusion Principle: Localize interface elements and simplify complex technical language.

06 · features

three pillars of safe organizing.

01community & group organizingLowering the barrier to civic participation without lowering safety.
  • Vetted onboarding: custom application questions let leads screen new members, balancing open access with collective safety
  • Initiative discovery hub: search local or category-specific groups
  • Role-based permissions: Lead Organizer, Trusted Planner, FAQ Responder and Content Moderator roles spread the work and reduce leader burnout
02secure communication & privacyEvery message protected, every footprint under the user's control.
  • End-to-end encryption: for group threads and private messages
  • Data retention controls: disappearing messages from 24 hours to 90 days, plus automated cleanup
  • Built-in moderation: members can flag harassment or security threats directly to group leads
03action & engagementTurning conversation into collective action.
  • Centralized dashboard: active groups, calendars and activity feeds in one panel to reduce cognitive load
  • Consensus builders: polls and petitions turn ongoing discussion into measurable, democratic action
  • Language localization: multi-language support and automatic tone simplification for non-native English speakers
07 · decisions

Nodo splits leadership into four roles. why not one admin per group? Burnout came up again and again in research. Granular roles let organizers share the operational weight instead of carrying it alone.
