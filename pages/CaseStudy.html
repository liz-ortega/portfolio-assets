02 · the challenge The green industry is broad and confusing.

Nontraditional job seekers and career-switchers face overwhelming interfaces, dense jargon, and real uncertainty about how their skills translate to sustainability roles.

03 · the shift

from gatekeeping to guiding.

rigid credential checks → exploration over evaluation.

Instead of testing whether people already qualify, the platform helps them discover where they fit, starting from their values and motivations, then showing approachable pathways in.

EXPLORATION · NOT SHIPPED

breaking my guide down, piece by piece.

Feedback: the moments where people add things weren’t fully designed. So I pulled out each piece, studied how familiar products handle it, and rebuilt it in my guide’s style.

PIECE 01the notepad: adding and uploadingThis is where people bring in what they find: links, files and notes. In my guide it's a button, so I broke the moment into four steps.
MY GUIDE TODAY
THE GAP“Add Notes” had nothing behind it.What opens on click? Can I drop a PDF? What does a saved link look like, and which tab does it go to?
HOW PEOPLE ALREADY DO THIS
LIKE REDDITOne “create” entry with tabs for the type of thing you’re posting, so there aren’t separate buttons for each.
LIKE SLACKThe composer is always there. Paste a link and it unfurls into a preview before you send.
LIKE PINTERESTSaving asks one question: which board? Saved things come back as visual cards in a grid.
LIKE GOOGLE DRIVEDrag a file anywhere and the whole area becomes a drop target, then each file shows its own progress.

06c · qa that shaped the product

testing as a job seeker, designing as I went.

Before launch, I tested Green Jobs Board 2.0 as a job seeker on Safari and Chrome and wrote a roadmap of bugs and UX fixes, each with a design solution. 15 of the 21 items I logged are now live.

I also logged 11 issues on the team’s launch tracker across job seeker, employer and admin views. Shipped: applying to mentor from Treehouse, saved job notes, a clearer edit profile button, readable tags and support text, support attachments, and skills on pathways.

ASSET-TO-JOB LINKINGGeneric resumes don't work anymore. A dropdown in Saved/Applied Jobs links the exact resume, cover letter or essay version to each job, giving a bird's-eye view of what was sent where.
JOB TRACKER, SAFERSHIPPEDPostings disappear. Keep notes on saved jobs, add a manual “date posted,” and get a heads-up when a saved listing passes 30 days.
CLEARER JOB LINKSSHIPPEDThe cover letter's link field was ambiguous, and links deleted themselves after a few clicks. Now the field explains itself and links persist.
PROFILE POLISHSHIPPEDA shorter cover image to match Pathways, plus fixes for broken LinkedIn links, a “Why climate” redirect loop and unsaved work experience.

07 · decisions

The quiz asks about values and motivations, like “Your ideal win at work looks like…”, not technical skills. why not a skills test? Interviews showed credential checks were exactly what made career-switchers feel shut out, so the quiz is framed around curiosity, not assessment.

10 · reflection we made a confusing industry feel approachable.

Exploration over evaluation. Framing discovery around curiosity instead of assessment changed how people engaged.

Design the system, not the screen. Designing the quiz and guides at the same time made me think about a whole experience, not one isolated interaction.

Design for longevity. Structured UX documentation and clean design tokens keep the platform healthy for future engineering handoffs.
