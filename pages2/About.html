Nice to meet you, I'm Liz!

Designer, social advocate and community builder studying Cognitive Science + Media Studies.

I grew up in Oklahoma City and landed in the design world by way of Cognitive Science and Media Studies at Pomona College. My majors are part scientist (the “why”), part engineer (the “how”), and 100% obsessed with human-centric hardware.

I started by leading a design team for a mental health nonprofit and learned how design can move the needle on things that matter. Since then, I've launched stress-buster kits for middle and high school students and designed custom merch for my college departments. But now I spend most of my spare time in the digital world!
